Getting Started
What is Sentinel Grid? ▾
Sentinel Grid is a virtual stock trading simulator for Indian markets. You get ₹10,00,000 in simulated cash to trade 25+ NSE-listed stocks with live-simulated prices — with zero real money involved. It's the safest way to learn investing before you risk actual capital.
Is Sentinel Grid free? ▾
Yes, 100% free — now and always. There are no premium tiers, no in-app purchases, and no subscription fees. We believe access to financial education should be universal.
How do I create an account? ▾
Visit the homepage and sign up with Google (fastest) or with your email and password. Fill in your details and you'll be taken straight into the app — no email verification needed.
Are the prices real? ▾
Prices are live-simulated — they start from real NSE base prices and then simulate realistic tick-by-tick movement using a random walk model. They are not a live feed of actual exchange prices, so Sentinel Grid should not be used for real trading decisions.
Trading & Orders
What order types are supported? ▾
Sentinel Grid supports three order types:
  • Market Order — executes immediately at the current simulated price.
  • Limit Order — you set a price; the order simulates at that price.
  • Stop-Loss (SL) — triggers a sell at a price you set to limit downside.
How is P&L calculated? ▾
Unrealised P&L is calculated as: (Current Price − Average Cost) × Shares Held. Realised P&L is locked in when you sell, calculated as: (Sell Price − Average Buy Cost) × Shares Sold. Both are shown in your dashboard.
Can I short sell? ▾
Not currently. Sentinel Grid supports long positions only — you must own shares before selling them. Short selling and derivatives (F&O) are on the roadmap for a future update.
Why can't I place an order? ▾
The most common reasons are:
  • Buying: Insufficient virtual cash for the order total.
  • Selling: You don't hold that stock, or you're trying to sell more shares than you own.
  • Quantity: The quantity field is empty or set to zero.
Check the order summary panel for the estimated amount before placing.
How do I reset my portfolio? ▾
Portfolio resets are not currently available in-app. If you'd like to start fresh, please email us at sentinelgrid.app@gmail.com with your account email and we'll reset your balance and positions manually.
Account & Profile
How do I reset my password? ▾
On the login page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your email. We'll send a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
Does my portfolio save across devices? ▾
Yes. Your portfolio, orders, watchlist, and cash balance are stored securely in the cloud via Supabase. Sign in on any device and your account is exactly as you left it.
How do I delete my account? ▾
Email us at sentinelgrid.app@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email. Under GDPR, we will process your request within 30 days and permanently delete all your data.
Privacy & Security
What data does Sentinel Grid collect? ▾
We collect only what's needed to run your account: your name, email, and trading activity (simulated only). We never collect payment information, bank details, or sell your data. See our full Privacy Policy.
Is my data GDPR-compliant? ▾
Yes. Sentinel Grid is fully GDPR-compliant. You have the right to access, rectify, export, or delete your data at any time. Our privacy policy outlines all your rights. Email sentinelgrid.app@gmail.com to exercise them.
How is my password stored? ▾
Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use Supabase Auth which hashes passwords with bcrypt — the industry-standard algorithm. We never have access to your original password.
