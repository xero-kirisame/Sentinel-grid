Why we built Sentinel Grid

Most Indians learn about the stock market by losing money on their first trade. We think that's backwards. Sentinel Grid gives you a fully realistic trading environment — live-simulated NSE prices, real order types, a P&L tracker, and a portfolio dashboard — with absolutely zero financial risk.

Whether you're a college student curious about equities, a professional looking to sharpen your strategy, or an experienced trader testing new approaches, Sentinel Grid is your proving ground.
